Lost something? Found something? Be honest, be kind, and help it get home.
  • Only claim something that is really yours. Describe it from memory, not by guessing.
  • Found something? Thank you! Please hand it in, or don’t keep it. Someone is having a rough day without it.
  • Be patient and kind. Finders are helping for free.
  • Never share someone’s ID, phone, or card details publicly.
  • Do not post false information, misleading updates, or abusive comments. This hurts trust and may lead to account restrictions or reporting.
  • Say thanks when you get your stuff back. It makes everyone want to help again.
Heads up: your name and college ID are saved with every claim. False claims, abusive comments, or harmful behavior may lead to restricted access, warnings, or reporting to the college administration.
